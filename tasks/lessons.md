# Lessons

- Characters cannot carry fine detail. A plate drawn in glyphs needs flat fills, hard edges and clear gaps; texture inside a shape turns to noise. Check it at full size before calling it done.
- A photograph rendered only as glyphs is unreadable at portrait size. Keep the picture dimmed underneath.
- Soumya wants the homepage compact. Featured work goes in a grid, and on a phone in short rows, not full-height sections.
- A logo or mark goes in small. Default to under 240px and low contrast; do not give it a hero-sized slot.
- Copy: plain statements in their own lowercase voice. No winking references in visible text; keep those for hidden places (konami, 404).
- Anything that follows the scroll position (the docking name) must be a CSS scroll-driven animation, not a scroll listener. Script lags a frame behind on phones and shows as jitter.
- On a phone, entrances must finish fast (under about a second) and the first screen needs one large thing, not a small figure above text.
- Do not replace an asset Soumya chose (the brain favicon) with a new design. Improve the existing one.
- Footer stays minimal: no activity feed, no clock.
